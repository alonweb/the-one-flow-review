THE ONE Screens, Version 2

▶ Click through it like the app Present it: the app alone

The yellow version’s screens with the drag-to-predict concept for every question, plus new screens for making an avatar from a selfie, in THE ONE’s own colours: near-black and plum, hot pink for the player’s choices and the main button, gold for points and the lock. Names and big numbers are set in Anton, as in the feed concept. The example player is Big M. The question phones are playable, and the buttons on the other match and avatar screens work.

Numbers follow the flow, with an exact hit now worth 7: 0 for your own choice, +2 for the crowd’s winner, +1 more for the right 10-point range, and 7 in all for the exact %. That is up to 7 points a question and 35 a match. The full scoring and the tie-breakers are still to be confirmed. The flow’s contact menu and prices stay.

Phones animate when they scroll into view. Replay the animation, under a screen’s notes, plays it again.

For developers: rare failure states

These states have no screens of their own. Each row says what the player should see and what the app has to do. Open marks a rule still to be decided.
