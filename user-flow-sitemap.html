Today

Open round → matchup hub; unfinished matchup → resume saved draft; both locked → round-complete summary. Between rounds → countdown.

Arena

Before both locks: activity and reactions only, plus Return to Today. After both locks → personalized direction. After close: voting closed; follow Results. Personalized access is proposed for confirmation.

Results

Before 11:00: prior results or a first-round empty state. 11:00–13:00 → waiting; publication → reveal; complete → round result.

Standings

Players and Contestants are separate views. Published season standings persist while the next round is live. Before the first result: no ranking yet.
