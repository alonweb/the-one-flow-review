Drag to predict

A playable concept for one question of THE ONE. The vote and the prediction happen on one screen, by dragging two icons onto the photos, or simply tapping the photos.

  1. Drag yourself (your profile icon) onto the woman you choose, or just tap her photo. Your vote.
  2. Drag the crowd onto the woman you think most players will choose, or tap her photo.
  3. ▶ Preview on each photo opens her story (her photos, one line about her) with See her stats; Contact me under her name opens her page.
  4. Answer the banner over her: pick the range, then the exact %. Lock it. No points show while you vote: they come with the results, when the match closes.

You can also tap an icon, then a photo. To change your mind, drag an icon onto the other woman, or tap her while the question banner is open.

Prototype. Photos and numbers are examples; the crowd’s real split is never shown before results.
